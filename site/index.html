About me

I’m Haiyang — also known as Eason. I work where investigation meets response.

At Seatrium, I handle L2 investigations for cases escalated by BlueVoyant’s L1 team. I use Microsoft Defender and Microsoft Sentinel to examine alerts and take account remediation actions when needed.
